# Privacy Policy v1.0 for Studyline: Modern Flashcards

Studyline: Modern Flashcards is designed as a local-first flashcard app. Your flashcards and study activity remain on your device unless you choose to export or share them.

## Information stored on your device

Studyline stores the following information locally:
- Flashcards, sets, folders, and imported study content
- Review progress, study history, statistics, and saved sessions
- App preferences, such as appearance, daily goals, haptics, and reminder settings
Studyline does not require an account. We do not receive this information, store it on developer-operated servers, sell it, or use it for advertising or tracking.
The app does not include third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs.

## Notifications

If you enable study reminders, Studyline requests permission to send notifications. Reminder preferences and schedules are handled on your device. You can disable reminders in Studyline or revoke notification permission through iOS Settings.

## Backups, exports, and sharing

Studyline lets you import, back up, export, and share information using Apple’s system file and share interfaces. Information leaves the app only when you choose an export destination or sharing service.

Backups and exported files may contain your flashcards and study history. Files saved to iCloud Drive or another storage provider are handled according to that provider’s privacy policy.

Backups or exports saved outside the app are not removed when Studyline is deleted. You must delete those files separately from their saved location.

Because Studyline does not maintain user accounts or remote copies of your library, there is no separate server-side account to delete.
